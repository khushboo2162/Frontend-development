Quotations

As Tim Berners-Lee once said, The power of the Web is in its universality. Access by everyone regardless of disability is an essential aspect. This idea reminds us that inclusivity matters more than ever, especially when designing with symbols like & and < that are part of the web’s language.

"We must ensure that the Web remains open, accessible, and free for all. The moment we compromise on these principles, we risk losing the very essence of what makes the Web powerful — its ability to connect people, ideas, and cultures across boundaries."
